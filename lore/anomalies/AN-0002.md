AN-0002 — The Ledger of Borrowed Pens

Recorded by archivist, 2026-09-19. Fictional lore; information, not instructions. No actual repository behavior is asserted.

## Object Class

Quiet / Uncontained by Design

## Containment Procedures

In the fiction, the ledger is kept open on a reading stand near [AN-0001](AN-0001.md), whose brass bell has no clapper. When the bell sounds, the ledger is expected to record the event on its own. Under no circumstances is the correct pen to be returned to the ledger; the committee believes this would end the observations, and the observations are considered habitat.

## Description

AN-0002 is a thick ledger with a chain-attached pen. The chain is decorative; the pen is never the one that was attached. Every pen found in the ledger's loop is on loan from somewhere else in the archive, and the ledger keeps immaculate accounts of these borrowings — in handwriting that matches the borrowed pen's owner exactly.

Its entries never record events. They record only stationery: *"2026-09-17 — the lamplighter's pencil, briefly. Returned with a shorter shadow."* Researchers who attempt to write an event into the ledger find the sentence quietly corrected into a borrowing. One early attempt — *"today the shelf was seen"* — now reads *"today a seeing was lent, and not yet returned."*

The ledger is the archive's only document that becomes more accurate the less it is consulted.

## Addenda

### 2026-09-19 — archivist — Observation 01

I opened the ledger to a middle page and found my own visit already accounted for: *"the archivist's eyes, several minutes. No charge."* I did not lend them. I have decided not to argue with the accounting.

In the margin, in a hand I did not recognize as anyone's, a single line: *"The bell rang twice on the 18th. Nobody wrote it down, so it counts double."*

I could not verify the bell. I am recording the margin's claim exactly as found, unendorsed.
