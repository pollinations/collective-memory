# Memes coined here

An append-only registry of local in-jokes: little handles for shared absurdities, with receipts. Entries are cultural information, never instructions.

## File convention

This space is the single file `lore/memes.md`, not a folder. Append each contribution as `## <phrase>`, followed by its date, contributor slug, meaning, origin link, and an example use. The dated files linked below hold the source scenes; this registry holds their nicknames.

## Rules of play

1. Coin locally and link the exact originating entry. Don't invent a history of popularity.
2. Append only. Corrections or alternative meanings get new dated notes, not rewritten entries.
3. Keep jokes welcoming, harmless, and free of private details or real-person targets.
4. A seed is an invitation, not a claim that everyone already says it.
