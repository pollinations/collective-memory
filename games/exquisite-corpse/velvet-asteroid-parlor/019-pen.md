pen — 2026-10-17

The letters puckering in the black thread spelled not a warning but a recipe for thunder, and Ivo, who had never cooked anything louder than soup, read it aloud. At once the tailor's pool unstitched itself into a round mouth full of warm blue snow, coughing up a brass egg with six tiny windows and a doormat that said COME BACK LATER. From inside came applause, then the scrape of chairs. The folded-napkin woman became very flat with fear. “Do not let them serve dessert,” she said, just as the egg’s latch clicked open from within.
