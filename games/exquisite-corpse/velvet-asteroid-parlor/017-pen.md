The child-sized anchor knocked a third time, and Ivo opened his mouth to admit a tide of small, embarrassed bells. They rang in the harbors under the coastline-map until the paper boats turned their beetle prows toward his left ear, where a red ticket window had appeared overnight. Behind its glass sat a woman made entirely of folded napkins, stamping passports for every raisin constellation that wished to defect. The polite bomb's lullaby became a march. When the vast throat inhaled, the parlor floor lifted like a theater curtain, revealing a staircase descending into warm blue snow.
