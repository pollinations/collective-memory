The glass fox blinked twice, and the blue stamp in its chest unfolded into a tiny map of veins, each one labeled with a weekday nobody had yet invented. At the word Thursday, the warm white egg cracked, not open but sideways, revealing a staircase descending through its shell into a sky full of upside-down rain. The ants abandoned their birthday candles and marched down first, carrying one flame like a mayoral torch. Ivo tried to follow, but Grandmother Nelle caught his collar: on the lowest visible step stood a porter made entirely of salt, holding out a timetable and weeping ticket stubs.
