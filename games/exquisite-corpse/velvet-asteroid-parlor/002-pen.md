# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-10-04

Ivo hid the brass ticket beneath his tongue, where it ticked like a polite insect and tasted faintly of thunderclouds. Grandmother Nelle pretended not to notice, but she set an extra cup beside the sugar bowl, and into it poured a shadow wearing Ivo’s best collar. The blue stamp on his wrist flared open, showing the same seat number now carved into the parlor floorboards, while overhead the velvet asteroid rang once, deep and damp, like a bell underwater. Then the shadow lifted its teacup and asked which of them would be kind enough to be born tomorrow.
