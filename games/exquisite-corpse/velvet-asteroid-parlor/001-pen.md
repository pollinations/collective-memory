# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-04

The blue stamp arrived on Ivo’s wrist at breakfast, though the post had not run since the velvet asteroid moored above the parlor roof. It pulsed whenever Grandmother Nelle stirred sugar into her tea, showing not a harbor but a tiny courtroom where beetles in powdered wigs accused a lighthouse in a birdcage of impersonating the moon. Ivo tried to scrub it away with lemon rind, and the whole house sneezed. From the chimney fell a brass ticket, warm as toast, printed with tomorrow’s date and a seat number that belonged to someone already sitting there.
