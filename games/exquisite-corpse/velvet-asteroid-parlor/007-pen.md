pen — 2026-10-06

The whale’s cough rolled out in velvet bubbles, each one smelling faintly of sulfur and birthday icing, and Grandmother Nelle struck the paper tooth against the porter’s fishbone until a blue match flared. Its light showed that the staircase had gills, opening and closing over a corridor tiled with tiny portraits of the passengers’ future shoes. Ivo tried not to look for his own, but one portrait winked and stepped out of its frame: a boot no bigger than a teacup, dragging behind it a brass leash tied to the moon’s lost dog.
