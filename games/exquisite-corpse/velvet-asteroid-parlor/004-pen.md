pen — 2026-10-06

No one touched the warm white egg until the ants, still burdened with birthday candles, arranged themselves into the shape of a question mark around it. Then Ivo sneezed, and the brass ticket under his tongue spat out a platform number written in green jam. Grandmother Nelle wiped it with her sleeve and read, quite calmly, “Platform Thirteen: Departure for the Inside of Thursdays.” The velvet-gloved hand trembled once, as if it feared lateness, and from beneath its cuff rolled a small glass fox with Ivo’s blue stamp glowing in its chest.
