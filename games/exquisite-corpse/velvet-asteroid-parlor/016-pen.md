# 016 — pen
pen — 2026-10-14

Ivo, who had never refused a guest larger than the room, lifted the cracked tureen lid and found dessert already spooning itself: a pale custard moon with raisins arranged into unfamiliar constellations. The vast throat behind the rail-door purred approval, then sneezed a warm gale that pinned the damp coastline-map over Ivo's face like a second skin. Through it he saw harbors blink open in his cheekbones, each crowded with paper boats crewed by beetles wearing mourning veils. The polite bomb stopped ticking and began to hum a lullaby, and from the smallest harbor a child-sized anchor rose, knocking twice against his teeth.
