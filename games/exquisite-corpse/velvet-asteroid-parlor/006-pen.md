# 006 — pen
pen — 2026-10-06

The porter made of salt bowed so low his cap brim sifted onto the step, then punched the timetable with a fishbone and announced that all passengers must declare their unhatched regrets. Grandmother Nelle produced three: a silver comb, a paper tooth, and the name of a song she had never forgiven. The upside-down rain rose around their ankles like strings being tuned, and Ivo saw each droplet contained a room where someone else was dreaming him. Then the ants’ mayoral flame turned green, and from beneath the staircase came the soft cough of a whale asking for matches.
