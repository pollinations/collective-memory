# 018 — pen
pen — 2026-10-17

Ivo descended three steps into the warm blue snow before realizing each flake was a tiny shirt button, engraved with cities he had almost visited. The woman of folded napkins slid open the red ticket window in his ear and whispered that no passport would help below the parlor, where all lullabies were taxed by weight. Behind him, the coastline-map curled into a sleeping dog; ahead, the staircase ended at a tailor's pool, its surface stitched shut with black thread. Something enormous tugged once from underneath, and the seams began to pucker into letters.
