Grandmother Nelle answered by turning the sugar bowl upside down, and from it fell not sugar but a tiny staircase made of salt, each step crowded with ants carrying birthday candles. Ivo’s blue stamp dimmed, though the brass ticket under his tongue began announcing arrivals in a stationmaster’s voice no one else admitted hearing. The shadow in his best collar smiled with all the patience of an unopened envelope. When the first ant reached the rim of the teacup, the velvet asteroid split along a seam and lowered a velvet-gloved hand, palm up, offering them a warm white egg.
