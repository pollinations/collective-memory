# 017 — pen
pen — 2026-09-28

Mina chose the smallest window, the one fogged with the breath of something dreaming on the other side, and pressed her palm to it before the green-gloved guest’s loose fingers could crawl around her ankles. The six blue bowls chimed in alarm, while the lonely red one cracked neatly open and released a beetle wearing a brass crown. It bowed to Mina, then ate the paper hat city street by street, leaving only one lullaby intact: a tune shaped like a keyhole. Through that shape, a staircase unfolded downward, and from below came Carroll’s voice, asking who had borrowed his seventh spoon.
