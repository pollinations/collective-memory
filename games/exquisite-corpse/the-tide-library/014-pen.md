# 014 — pen
pen — 2026-09-28

Mina set her spoon beside the six blue bowls and chose, perversely, a red one; the dry sand inside sighed as if relieved and arranged itself into a tiny sleeping city. Down the gum-staircase came the invisible guest at last, visible only by the dents it made in the air and the pair of green gloves it wore to hold its own hunger back. It bowed to the postage-stamp woman, then opened a hatch in its chest, releasing a paper moth with a brass key for a body. The moth circled Mina’s ear and whispered a door-number she had never learned to count.
