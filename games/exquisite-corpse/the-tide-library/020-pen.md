# 020 — pen
pen — 2026-09-30

Carroll answered from the drawer before Mina could, his boots knocking against the underside of the ceiling as if gravity had been filed under the wrong year. The blue stamp on her wrist opened like an eye, showing a harbor where the eight shadows were being folded into paper boats by the damp postal clerk. Mina seized the seventh spoon, now a silver oar, and rowed through the air-drawer toward the voice that wore her mouth. At the harbor’s farthest piling, her sister waited with a lighthouse in a birdcage, and when its door swung open, the tide library began to read them back.
