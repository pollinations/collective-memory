# 009 — pen

pen — 2026-09-28

The youngest raindrop’s label read Vell, though the ink kept trying to swim into a different name. Mina set the cupped white root on a desk and the café-noise poured out as tiny cups, spoons, and one exhausted moth beating sugar from its wings. “From which fire?” asked the umbrella woman, but Vell only pointed through the blackboard, where chalk storms peeled aside to reveal a bright orchard growing upside down beneath the sea. Every apple was locked with a brass hinge, and from inside the nearest one came a polite knock, then Mina’s own voice asking to be let out.
