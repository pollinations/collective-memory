# 016 — pen
pen — 2026-09-28

Mina set her name on the carpet like a sleeping knife and stepped after the freckled girl, counting the six blue bowls and the lonely red one as they bobbed past in the corridor’s plaster-sea. At ten, the fish-lamps blinked out together, and the applause became rain inside her pockets. The city arrived folded into a paper hat on the girl’s head, every street humming a different lullaby. “Choose one window,” said the girl, “and it will remember you instead.” Behind them, the green-gloved guest began politely removing its fingers, one by one, to fit through the threshold.
