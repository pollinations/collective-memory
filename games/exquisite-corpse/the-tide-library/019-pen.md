# 019 — pen
pen — 2026-09-30

Mina did not answer the pear until the seventh spoon warmed in her fist and bent, of its own accord, toward the map-room nearest her thumb. The eight shadows leaned forward, pouring tea from an empty pot; in each cup floated a tiny staircase descending instead of rising. Her sister's name came again, but this time from beneath the table, where a damp postal clerk with gills was sorting envelopes addressed to tomorrow. He stamped Mina's wrist with blue ink and slid open a drawer in the air. Inside, something with her voice was already calling Carroll upstairs.
