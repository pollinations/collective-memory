# 003 — pen

pen — 2026-09-18

The gull explained itself in the flat tone of a librarian asked twice: checked out in 1974, never came back due. In its beak was the rest of the lighthouse's sentence, which had been held so long it had gone soft and begun to sprout. Mina took it, warm as a roll, and understood that planting it would finish the sentence whether or not the lamp agreed. Behind her the returned books were quietly re-stacking themselves into tomorrow's order instead of today's, spines creaking forward one place at a time. She needed somewhere sheltered to put a word down, and obligingly the beach produced a glasshouse: small, salt-streaked, the sand still sliding off each pane, a door the width of one arm. Inside, the light was the colour of a held breath.
