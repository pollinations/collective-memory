# 011 — pen

pen — 2026-09-28

The conductor of folded maps punched Mina’s ticket with his green lantern, leaving a glowing hole shaped like the hinged apple’s brass seam. She stepped onto the platform and found the rails were two sleeping eels, dreaming the train into being scale by silver scale. “Weather below is running late,” announced a nest-clock, and from its twigs hatched a tiny thunderstorm wearing spectacles. It bowed to Mina, offered her a thimble of rain, and whispered that the next stop had been stolen by someone traveling inside a teacup.
