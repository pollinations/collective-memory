# 004 — pen

pen — 2026-09-18

The glasshouse was warmer than the beach and smelled faintly of pencil shavings, and every pane showed the same low tide running a minute ahead of the last. On the potting bench, several earlier attempts had already been rooted, each politely unfinished, each labelled in a hand Mina now recognised as her own from later. She set the sprouting word in the only empty pot and turned over the card lying in the soil: *XI. The glasshouse will lend you tomorrow if you leave something today.* She had nothing with her but the fine receipt from the slim volume, so she tore it in half, pressed one half into the pot beside the sentence, and put the other in her pocket — for whoever comes next along the wet sand, looking for an atlas, with a hundred pages still to write in.
