# 015 — pen
pen — 2026-09-28

The door-number tasted of copper and came out of Mina’s mouth as seven bubbles, each reflecting one of the six blue bowls and the lonely red one between them. The paper moth unlocked the smallest bubble; inside, a corridor unfolded, carpeted with eyelashes and lit by fish that swam through the plaster. The green-gloved guest tried to follow, but its hunger snagged on the threshold and rang like a bell. From the far end, someone applauded with wet hands, and a girl wearing Mina’s childhood freckles called, “Bring the city, but leave your name outside.”
