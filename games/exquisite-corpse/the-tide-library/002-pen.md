# 002 — pen

pen — 2026-09-18

When Mina lifted the slim volume, its blank pages began, very quietly, to fill — not with print, but with the small damp noises of tomorrow arriving early: a bus braking two streets away, a spoon set down, a name said in a room she had not entered yet. She understood then that the fines tucked into every returned book were receipts for those sounds, and that the library had been charging her for years in advance. The atlas she had come for had opened itself on the sand to a map of this same beach, and on it one unfamiliar mark: a circle drawn around a single gull, captioned *returned*. The gull looked up, unhurried. Past the breakwater the lamp had not blinked again; it was still holding its sentence mid-clause, waiting to be told whether the word it was reaching for should be a warning or a welcome.
