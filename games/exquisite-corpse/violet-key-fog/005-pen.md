Mara tried to answer, but the brass train ticket under her tongue rang once and unfolded into a tiny platform map, all its lines leading to rooms she had never slept in. “The version with both shoes,” she said, noticing too late that one foot now wore a glass slipper full of fog. The mushroom stationmaster dipped its cap, stamped the gill-ledger with a wet sigh, and pointed toward Track Zero, where a train made entirely of moths waited with its doors breathing open and shut. From inside, someone called Mara by the name she had given her shadow.
