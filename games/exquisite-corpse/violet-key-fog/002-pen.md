# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-11-20

Mara did not open the letter at first; she held it to her ear, as she did with seashells and stubborn jam jars, and heard tomorrow pacing inside like a moth in a matchbox. The black root curled around her ankle with surprising gentleness, spelling IVO in soil on the toe of her boot, then tugged once toward the parlor chair’s foggy cushion. When she finally broke the seal, a warm rain of canary feathers fell out, and among them a brass train ticket punched for a station that had not yet been built.
