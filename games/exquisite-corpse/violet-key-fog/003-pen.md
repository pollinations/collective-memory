# 003 — pen
pen — 2026-11-24

Mara tucked the brass train ticket beneath her tongue, because tomorrow, when cornered, could sometimes be bribed by silence. The canary feathers rose from the floor and arranged themselves into a tiny yellow courtroom, complete with a judge who was only a thimble wearing spectacles. It struck the parlor chair’s foggy cushion with a spoon and declared the station guilty of arriving late to its own construction. At that, the ticket warmed, and through the window Mara saw rails unspool across the sky toward a platform where someone in her coat was already waiting.
