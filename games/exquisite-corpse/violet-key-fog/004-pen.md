The woman in Mara’s coat lifted a hand, and Mara felt her own elbow answer from across the sky. She kept the brass train ticket under her tongue until its heat tasted of pepper and bells, then stepped through the window onto the first rail, which sagged like a wet clothesline beneath her shoes. Behind her, the thimble judge squeaked an objection, but the platform ahead had already begun to bloom with white mushrooms wearing stationmaster caps. One of them opened a ledger made of gills and asked which version of Mara would be checking in.
