The wet envelope would not open until Aram put one of the unborn gloves on his left hand; then its seal sighed like a sleeper and unfolded into a red alley no wider than a ribbon. Mara stepped into it first and became briefly transparent, all her bones labeled in careful gold script. The saucer-moth settled on Aram's shoulder as stalls behind them began selling the exact sounds they had just made, bottled and corked. Halfway down the alley, a fountain coughed up a brass egg with a window in it, and inside the window someone was waving Mara's face.
