The black-salt snowman licked its charcoal thumb and turned a page already crowded with Aram's childhood mistakes, each written in Mara's borrowed handwriting. NOTYET climbed the ledger's spine and rang there like a small locked bell. “You owe one future,” the snowman said, and from its hollow chest produced a green mitten full of sleeping bees. Mara, or the face wearing her, began to laugh backward until the red alley paled to dawn. Then the saucer-moth spat out a perfect little door it had chewed from Aram's collar, and something behind it knocked twice.
