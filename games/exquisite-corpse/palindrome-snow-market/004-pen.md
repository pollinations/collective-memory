Mara's borrowed face in the brass egg stopped waving and pressed one finger to its lips, though the face outside the windowless shell still belonged to Mara, sweating in the red alley. Aram lifted the egg; it was warm as a recently told lie, and the saucer-moth began gnawing tiny commas into his collar. From the labeled bones under Mara's skin, a golden word detached itself and crawled across the cobbles: NOTYET. Every bottle in the sound-stalls uncorked at once, releasing their footsteps backward, and at the far end of the alley a snowman made of black salt opened a ledger.
