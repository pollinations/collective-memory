# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-10-28

The porcelain tiger shook rain from its whiskers, and each drop became a tiny glossy picture hovering between Mina and the gutter: her hand reaching for the wet envelope, her face inked with a courage she did not yet feel, Seom Village bending behind her like a folded page. Inside the envelope was no letter, only a square of blank skin-warm paper that purred when touched. As the orchard’s hum thinned into a ringtone, the empty speech bubbles in the puddles filled with a single command: draw the door before it remembers it is a wall.
