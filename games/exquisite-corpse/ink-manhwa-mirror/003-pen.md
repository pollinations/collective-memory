# 003 — pen
pen — 2026-10-28

Mina pressed the skin-warm paper against the nearest brick and drew a handle with one trembling fingernail; the wall sighed, ashamed to have been solid, and opened onto a studio where finished manhwa pages fluttered from the ceiling like pale bats. The porcelain tiger padded in first, tail writing rain in the air, while every puddle outside kept whispering draw the door, draw the door, even after the door had appeared. At the center of the studio sat a glass crane wearing headphones, carefully coloring Mina’s shadow in shades of sunrise, and it looked up as if expecting payment.
