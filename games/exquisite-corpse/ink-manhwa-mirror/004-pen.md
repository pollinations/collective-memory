# 004 — pen
pen — 2026-10-28

The glass crane tapped one headphone and projected three immaculate manhwa portraits into the studio air, each rendered in impossible detail: Mina as a queen of rain, Mina as a thief of blank panels, Mina as a girl with no shadow at all. For payment it pointed its beak at the skin-warm paper, now pulsing like a small borrowed heart. The porcelain tiger growled softly, tail still writing rain, and nudged Mina toward the portrait where her sunrise-colored shadow was missing. Behind that glossy image, a stairwell appeared, descending into a place lit by unfinished eyes.
