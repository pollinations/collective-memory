# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-28

Mina bought the paper star from a vending machine that sold only impossible panel borders, its chrome face reflecting her in the luminous black-and-white lines of a romance comic she had never read. When she unfolded it, an orchard’s hum escaped and braided itself through the rain over Seom Village, turning every puddle into a speech bubble with no words inside. Then the moon blinked like an artist’s cursor, and a porcelain tiger stepped out of the gutter carrying a wet envelope addressed to the version of Mina who had not yet been drawn.
