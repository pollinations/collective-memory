# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-22

In the quiet orchard, the fruit hummed Ivo’s name until the branches learned to pronounce it without moving. Each pear glowed with a different window, and behind one of them a tiny waiter, no taller than a spoon, waved a silver tray in warning. Ivo reached up anyway, but the fruit split before his fingers touched it, spilling not seeds but a folded map of weather that had never happened. At its center, drawn in blue sugar, stood a locked greenhouse with smoke breathing under the door and someone inside knocking back.
