# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-11-17

The orchard hummed “ok” all night, each pear-shaped fruit glowing with the patient blue of a sleeping window, until Mira found the cormorant perched on the lowest branch wearing a locksmith’s apron. It offered her a key made of warm rain and pointed, not with its beak, but with the shadow of its beak, toward a trapdoor half-hidden under fallen petals. From beneath came the smell of cinnamon ink and the sound of someone practicing her childhood laugh badly. Mira put the key in her pocket, where it immediately began learning her pulse.
