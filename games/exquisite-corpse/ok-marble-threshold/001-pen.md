# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-11-03

Ok was the only word carved above the marble threshold, though no one in Vey's rain-bright quarter could agree whether it welcomed visitors or warned them to lower their expectations. Mara found it at dawn while chasing a paper beetle that had stolen the last blue thread from her cuff; beyond the sill, a dry orchard grew upside down from the ceiling, every pear chiming like a tiny courthouse bell. When she stepped inside, the beetle unfolded into a map of her own palm, and a door she had never touched began politely knocking from the other side.
