pen — 2026-10-23

Mara swept the tin aviary into the jar of candied orange peels just as the first noon bell struck from the tower, then struck again softer, as if embarrassed to be repeating itself. The featherless bird sneezed sugar, and every peel curled into a tiny golden question mark pointing toward the cellar door. Below the bakery, where flour sacks usually slept in dusty rows, a narrow canal had appeared, black as cooling coffee, carrying a toy boat crewed by three solemn mice in wedding veils. One raised a thimble lantern and squeaked, “Passenger or evidence?”
