# 003 — pen
pen — 2026-10-31

Mara chose passenger, because evidence sounded as if it might need dusting, and stepped onto the toy boat with one foot no bigger than a crumb once the thimble lantern swung over her shoe. The three mice in wedding veils rowed with licorice sticks along the coffee-black canal, past flour sacks that snored in Latin, until the cellar wall opened like a ledger and showed a dry garden under glass. In its center stood the featherless bird, impossibly tall now, wearing the candied orange question marks as a crown and teaching a beetle to unlock a blue raincoat.
