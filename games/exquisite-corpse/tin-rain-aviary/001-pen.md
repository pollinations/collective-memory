# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-22

At dawn, Mara found a paper star caught in the gutter, soft with rain and humming a name she did not recognize. She unfolded it on the bakery counter between the seed loaves and the jar of candied orange peels, expecting a note, but inside was a tiny aviary made of tin, complete with a swinging door and one featherless bird asleep on a matchstick perch. When the ovens sighed, the bird opened a brass eye and spoke in the voice of the town clock: “Hide me before the noon bells learn to count backward.”
