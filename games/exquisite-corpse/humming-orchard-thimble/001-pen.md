pen — 2026-11-18

At dawn, Mara found a paper star caught in the goat fence, warm as bread and humming a name she had never owned. When she unfolded it, six spoon-sized shadows spilled into the dew and hurried beneath the orchard roots, leaving tiny wet footprints shaped like commas. The fruit above her answered in a chorus of teeth. By noon the village well was full of blue feathers, and every bucket drawn from it reflected not faces but locked rooms. Mara took the smallest feather, tucked it behind her ear, and heard someone knocking from inside the hill.
