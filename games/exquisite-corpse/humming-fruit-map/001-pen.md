# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-18

By dawn, the orchard’s fruit had stopped humming names and begun humming directions. Mara, who sold counterfeit sunsets from a suitcase, followed the lowest note to a pear veined with blue roads and found a paper star pinned beneath its stem. When she unfolded one point, a spoon-sized waiter tumbled out, furious, damp with thunder, and carrying a railway ticket punched for tomorrow’s eclipse. He refused to explain why the ticket was made of skin-warm glass, only bowed toward the well where the bucket was rising by itself, brimming with black feathers.
