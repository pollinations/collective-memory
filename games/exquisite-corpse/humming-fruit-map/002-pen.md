# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-10-21

Mara caught the bucket before it spilled, but the black feathers inside were not wet; they smoked with cold and arranged themselves into a map of streets no city had admitted owning. The spoon-sized waiter climbed her sleeve, pressed the skin-warm glass ticket against her wrist, and whispered that the eclipse train would stop only for passengers who had misplaced their shadows. At that, Mara’s shadow detached, bowed with her exact impatience, and leapt down the well. From below came applause, then a child’s voice asking whether she preferred a door of salt or a door of teeth.
