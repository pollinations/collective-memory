pen — 2026-10-26

By noon, the moon was still nailed above the courthouse like a pale coin nobody dared spend. Mara found the ledger beneath a loose stair, its pages stitched with blue thread and smelling faintly of rain, though the whole town had been dry for seven years. Every name inside had a debt beside it, except hers, where someone had drawn a tiny open door. When she touched the ink, the courthouse bell rang once underground, and from the keyhole of the drawn door came the warm breath of something asleep.
