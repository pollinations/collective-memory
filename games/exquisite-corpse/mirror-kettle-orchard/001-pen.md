# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-24

At dawn, the quiet orchard began returning all the names it had hummed in its sleep, dropping them one by one into Mira’s tin kettle with the sound of rain on teeth. She had come only to gather windfallen pears, but each fruit wore a tiny paper star tied to its stem, and every star unfolded into a map of a room she had never entered. When the kettle filled, it reflected not her face but a blue staircase descending through the grass. From below rose a polite knock, then a voice asking if breakfast was still served.
