# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-31

The first fruit to hum Mara’s name was pear-shaped, blue as a bruise, and hanging from a branch that had not been there when she locked the observatory. Inside its song she heard the scratch of a tiny cartographer sharpening his pencil. She plucked it, and the peel unrolled into a paper star marked with rivers that flowed uphill, a city labeled ONLY WHILE ASLEEP, and one blank island breathing softly in the corner. Then the telescope coughed out a warm brass key, still wet with seawater, and something below the floor knocked three times.
