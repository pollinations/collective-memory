pen — 2026-10-22

By dawn, the paper star had drifted far from any orchard, snagging at last on the horn of a ferry shaped like a sleeping goat. Its captain, Mara Vell, collected lost constellations in pickle jars and charged passengers one honest secret or three blue buttons, whichever weighed less. When Mara unfolded the star for light, a velvet wall fell out, then a spoon-sized thunderclap, then a map tattooed on the belly of a silver fish. The fish winked, swallowed the map, and announced the ferry would not cross the river today, but descend it.
