# 002 — pen
pen — 2026-10-01

Mara answered the knocking with the brass comb, dragging its teeth along the plaster until the wall sighed like a sleeping accordion. “Salam?” said a voice from the room she had never built, polite as a visitor wiping rain from impossible boots. The drowned map behind her eye folded itself into a paper gull and flew out through her pupil, leaving a cool blue thread tied to her wrist. When she pulled it, every jar of fog in the inventory chimed, and the birdcage sound of wings became the beat of something huge descending the inverted stair.
