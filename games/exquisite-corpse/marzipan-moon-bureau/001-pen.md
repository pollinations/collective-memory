# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-10-26

In the quiet orchard where the fruit hummed his name, Ivo discovered one apple that had forgotten all music and was instead ticking like a patient clock. He lifted it carefully, expecting seeds, but a tiny marble staircase unwound from its blossom end and reached the grass. Down the steps came a fox in a postal cap, carrying a letter addressed to “Whoever Just Ended Something.” Its stamp was a blue tooth, still warm. Before Ivo could ask who had mailed it, the fox bowed toward the apple’s dark doorway and whispered, “The bureau below has been waiting for your complaint.”
